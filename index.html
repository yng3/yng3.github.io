The local candidate demonstrated signed result receipts alongside consent checks and budget settlement. Its reported test suite passed 727 tests, including wrong-key rejection, retry consistency, and transactional rollback.

A signed receipt establishes that the relevant signing key was used. It does not by itself prove physical machine identity or remote execution. An authorized host and authenticated transport remain separate requirements before online deployment.
