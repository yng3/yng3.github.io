A signed claim is only useful if another party can check it. This local demonstration verifies synthetic request and receipt signatures, then shows what happens when the recorded result changes.

TEST VECTORS ONLY. The disposable public keys are bundled with the example. Their verification establishes self-consistency, not independently trusted identity, consent, execution, host attestation, or a financial charge. This is not connected to QUINN's operational worker.

VERIFYING TEST VECTOR

Proof has boundaries.

The signatures demonstrate that the bundled keys signed these particular bytes. Request and result hashes establish that the sample claims remain unchanged. They cannot show whether a real worker ran a task, whether a human approved it, whether a host is genuine, or whether any money was spent. External trust and independently verified operational records are future integration requirements.
